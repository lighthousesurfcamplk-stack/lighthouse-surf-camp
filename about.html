Our Story

Born on this coast.
Made for surfers.

Light House Surf Camp is a local-run camp in Arugam Bay, built by surfers who grew up reading these waves. We share the breaks we love with travellers from all over the world.

Who We Are

A surf camp with local soul.

Arugam Bay is more than a destination to us — it's home. We grew up surfing Main Point, learning the moods of Pottuvil and discovering hidden gems like the Light House break at Sangamankanda. After the war, as surfers from around the world began discovering this paradise, we wanted to share it the right way: with genuine local knowledge, real care, and warm Sri Lankan hospitality.

Today we run surf lessons, all-inclusive camps, board rentals and coaching for everyone from first-timers to advanced surfers — and we handle the whole trip, from airport pick-up to your last sunset session.

Beyond The Waves

We handle the whole adventure.

Surfing is the heart of it, but Arugam Bay has so much more. We organise Kumana National Park safaris, Pottuvil lagoon canoe trips, visits to ancient temples like Muhudu Maha Viharaya, sunset hikes up Elephant Rock, yoga, fishing trips and nights out on the buzzing main street.
