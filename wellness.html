Signature Treatments

The healing arts.

Each therapy is prescribed individually by Dr. Swarna based on your constitution and intentions. A selection of what's available:

🌿

Abhyanga

A grounding warm-oil massage of the head, face, body and feet. Calms the nervous system, improves circulation and prepares the body for deeper therapies.

💧

Shirodhara

A continuous, rhythmic stream of warm medicated oil poured over the third eye. Profoundly calming — especially for stress, insomnia and anxiety.

♨️

Swedana

Herbal steam therapy that opens the channels, loosens deep-seated toxins and lets medicinal herbs penetrate the tissues. Deeply relaxing after massage.

🌾

Kizhi

Warm herbal poultices pressed and massaged over the body — generating therapeutic heat that loosens stiff joints, revives muscles and boosts circulation.

🪷

Panchakarma Detox

Ayurveda's complete bio-purification — a guided multi-day sequence of cleansing therapies to eliminate toxins and rebalance body, mind and spirit. On request.

🍵

Ayurvedic Nutrition

Three dosha-balanced meals and herbal drinks prepared as medicine — the timeless art of eating in alignment with your body and the seasons.
