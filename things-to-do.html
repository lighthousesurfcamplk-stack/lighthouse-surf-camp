Beyond the Waves

Things to do in
Arugam Bay.

Ranked among the world's ten best surf destinations, Arugam Bay is so much more than its waves — a wild east coast of empty beaches, lagoons full of wildlife, ancient temples and elephant-dotted national parks. Here's how to fill the hours between sessions.

Arugam Bay at a Glance

A coastline that teaches, too.

On Sri Lanka's east coast, Arugam Bay is a true tropical paradise — the best surf conditions, clean white-sand beaches, crimson sunsets and a warm, welcoming culture. What makes it special is the variety: around ten surf spots within a 30-minute tuk-tuk ride, and just as many adventures inland and along the coast.

June to October is peak season, when the waves are at their best and the bay comes alive. We'll help you arrange every excursion — just ask at the camp.

Adventures & Experiences

Fill your days.

From dawn safaris to sunset hikes — a few of our favourite ways to explore. All bookable locally through the camp.

Wildlife

Kumana Safari

A half-day jeep safari — elephants, leopards and thousands of birds across 35,000 hectares.

Sunset

Elephant Rock

A short climb to the finest sunrise and sunset views in the whole bay.

Eco Tour

Pottuvil Lagoon

Canoe through mangroves to spot crocodiles, monitors and abundant birdlife.

Culture

Kudumbigala Monastery

A forest monastery with caves, trails and sweeping coastal views from the peak.

Wildlife

Lahugala National Park

A small elephant sanctuary — a 150-strong herd gathers at the watering hole at sunset.

Heritage

Muhudu Maha Viharaya

A 2,000-year-old beachside temple of ancient stupas and statues, magic at golden hour.

Wellness

Yoga & Wellness

Sunrise yoga, Ayurveda and sound healing — explore our full wellness menu.

Local Life

Lagoon Fishing

Join local fishermen on the lagoon for a relaxed, scenic glimpse of traditional methods.

Shopping

Main Street

Souvenirs, custom Arugam Bay clothing and great-value finds along the bustling main street.

After Dark

Night Life

Beach bars, buffets, bonfires and the freshest seafood served late into the night.

Food

Local Cuisine

Sri Lankan rice & curry, hoppers and fresh seafood — prepared with love.

Golden Hour

Sunrise & Sunset

East-coast sunrises straight from the sea, and unforgettable beach sunsets.

Begin & End Your Day

Catch the sunrise, chase the sunset.

Being a bay on the east coast, watching the sun rise straight out of the ocean is not only easy — it's magnificent. Head to the beach just before dawn to find your spot, and end the day with golden light from Elephant Rock. The perfect bookends to a day in paradise.
