Choose Your Level

Lessons for every stage of the journey.

🌱

Beginner Lessons

Never surfed? We start on the sand with theory, safety and pop-up technique, then get you standing on real, gentle waves at Baby Point or Whisky Point. Most students ride their first wave on day one.

  • ✓ Ocean safety & etiquette
  • ✓ Pop-up & balance drills
  • ✓ Soft-top board included
🌊

Intermediate Lessons

Standing up but want more? We work on paddling power, wave selection, trimming and your first proper turns on green waves, progressing you toward steeper point breaks.

  • ✓ Reading the lineup
  • ✓ Bottom turns & cutbacks
  • ✓ Board upgrade options
🎯

Private Coaching

One-on-one coaching focused entirely on you — the fastest way to fix bad habits and unlock the next level. Ideal before or during a surf trip.

  • ✓ 1-to-1 attention
  • ✓ Wave reading & technique
  • ✓ Personalised plan

How It Works

What a lesson day looks like.

  • 1 Meet & brief — we check the swell and pick the right beach for your level that day.
  • 2 Sand session — warm-up, safety, pop-up and paddling technique.
  • 3 In the water — your coach is right beside you, pushing waves and giving live feedback.
  • 4 Debrief — what you nailed, what to work on, and how to keep progressing.

The Lineup

Ten world-class breaks.

Within a 30-minute tuk-tuk ride lies a wave for every level. We read the swell each morning and take you to the one that fits you best.

Advanced

Main Point

A 50–150m point break over boulders — long, consistent rights and Arugam Bay's signature wave.

Beginner

Baby Point

A gentle 1m right-hander over sandy shallows — the safest place to catch your very first wave.

Beginner+

Whisky Point

Mellow, user-friendly waves breaking off a rocky point — perfect for building confidence.

Intermediate

Pottuvil Point

A stellar right-hander offering rides up to 1000m — pure barrel-and-carve territory.

Secret Spot

Light House

Sri Lanka's east extreme at Sangamankanda — empty white sand and peaks up to 7ft on a good swell.

All Levels

Elephant Rock

Tranquil dawn and dusk sessions with the most photogenic sunset views in the bay.

All Levels

Peanut Farm

A hidden gem tucked behind a lagoon 8km south — a beautiful beach and point break combo.

Intermediate

Okanda

A robust, sand-bottomed beach break near a low headland — exhilarating waves for every level.

Intermediate

Panama Point

Consistent waves for surfers who've surpassed the beginner stage and want to elevate their skills.
