The Accommodation

Hand-built. Hidden in green.

Every cabana and room at Light House is crafted from timber, natural-wood and thatch, raised gently among the palms. Fall asleep to the sound of the surf and wake to birdsong and ocean light.

Cabanas & Rooms

Seven ways to stay.

Sea-view tree-house cabanas, private rooms, a mountain cabana, a beachfront cabana and a bright mixed dorm — all hand-built from natural wood and coconut thatch, with fresh linens, mosquito nets and breakfast included.

Sea View · From $15 / night

Tree House Cabana Sea View

Our signature elevated timber A-frame, raised among the palms with sweeping views of the Indian Ocean from your own private deck. Fall asleep to the surf and wake to the horizon. The most romantic way to stay.

  • ✓ Double bed · sleeps 2
  • ✓ Elevated sea-view deck
  • ✓ Fan, mosquito net & fresh linens
Sea View · From $20 / night

Triple Room Tree House Sea View

A larger tree-house cabana sleeping three, with the same beautiful ocean outlook and private deck. Ideal for friends travelling together or small families who want space without compromising the view.

  • ✓ Sleeps 3 · double + single
  • ✓ Sea-view deck & jungle surrounds
  • ✓ Private bathroom · en-suite
Sea View · From $15 / night

Single Cabana Sea View

A cosy private cabana made for solo travellers, with a peaceful sea outlook and your own quiet corner of the camp. All the comfort and privacy of your own space, at a friendly price.

  • ✓ Single bed · sleeps 1
  • ✓ Sea outlook & private deck
  • ✓ Fan, mosquito net & fresh linens
Sea View · From $20 / night

Cabana Double Room Sea View

A comfortable double cabana opening onto a sea-view terrace strung with a hammock — the perfect spot to watch the morning sets roll in with a coffee in hand. Relaxed, private and right by the water.

  • ✓ Double bed · sleeps 2
  • ✓ Ocean-view terrace & hammock
  • ✓ Private bathroom · en-suite
Dorm · From $10 / night

Bed in 6-Bed Mixed Dormitory

Travelling solo or on a budget? Take a single bed in our bright, breezy 6-bed mixed dorm — vaulted timber ceiling, mosquito nets and big windows onto the green. The most social way to stay, and a great way to meet fellow surfers from around the world.

  • ✓ Single bed · 6-bed mixed dorm
  • ✓ Shared bathroom & common areas
  • ✓ Mosquito nets & fresh linens
Premium · From $50 / night

Mountain Cabana

Our premium hideaway, set a little higher with a private hammock terrace and long views over the treetops to the ocean beyond. Spacious, secluded and serene — the place to truly switch off.

  • ✓ Double bed · sleeps 2
  • ✓ Private hammock terrace & long views
  • ✓ Private bathroom · en-suite
Beachfront · From $25 / night

Buffalo Rock Cabana

A characterful timber cabana tucked among the trees at Buffalo Rock, moments from the sand. Relaxed and great value, with the surf and the beach right on your doorstep.

  • ✓ Double bed · sleeps 2
  • ✓ Steps from Buffalo Rock beach
  • ✓ Private bathroom · en-suite
