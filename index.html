The Retreat

An untamed paradise, thoughtfully tamed.

Tucked between palm forest and the Indian Ocean, Light House Surf Camp is a hideaway for those who want their adventure with a side of serenity. Our timber and thatch cabanas rise naturally from the jungle, our natural-wood restaurant frames the sunset, and the surf is never more than a barefoot stroll away.

It is the rare place where you can chase barrels at dawn, practise yoga at noon, and watch the stars from a hammock by night — all without leaving home.

  • ✓ Hand-built jungle cabanas & sea-view rooms
  • ✓ Beachfront natural-wood restaurant & bar
  • ✓ Surf lessons, coaching & board rentals
  • ✓ Safaris, yoga, and east-coast sunsets

The Place

Sri Lanka's wild east, all to yourself.

We sit near Sangamankanda — the eastern tip of Sri Lanka — where the beaches run empty and white, the swell rolls in clean, and the crowds of the main bay feel a world away. Around us: lagoons full of wildlife, ancient temples, elephant-dotted national parks and the most magnificent sunrises on the island.

  • ✓ Kumana & Lahugala safaris, Pottuvil lagoon trips
  • ✓ Elephant Rock sunsets & temple visits
  • ✓ Airport transfers, tuk-tuks & bikes arranged

Before You Book

Everything travellers ask us.

Peak surf season runs June to October, when the swell is most consistent and the bay is at its best. Our cabanas are limited and fill first for those months, so book early. Outside the peak the coast is quieter, greener and ideal if you are learning.

Yes — most of our guests arrive as complete beginners. Lessons run in small groups with certified local coaches, and we read the swell each morning to pick a break that suits your level. Soft-tops and rash guards are provided.

You reserve with a 25% deposit online and settle the balance on arrival in Arugam Bay. Cancellation is free up to 14 days before check-in. If you would rather not pay online at all, message us on WhatsApp and we will arrange it with you directly.

Your cabana, every meal, your coached surf sessions and your board hire — all in one price. Longer camps add Ayurvedic treatments, video coaching and surf-safari trips up the coast. The full list sits on each package card above.

Tell us your flight and we will arrange the transfer. Tuk-tuks and bikes can be organised once you are here, so you can explore the coast at your own pace.

Always. Vegetarian and vegan options are available at every meal, alongside authentic Sri Lankan rice and curry, hoppers and the daily catch. Tell us about any allergies when you book and the kitchen will plan around them.
