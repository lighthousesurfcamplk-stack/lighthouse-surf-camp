The Retreat

An untamed paradise, thoughtfully tamed.

Tucked between palm forest and the Indian Ocean, Light House Surf Camp is a hideaway for those who want their adventure with a side of serenity. Our timber and thatch cabanas rise naturally from the jungle, our natural-wood restaurant frames the sunset, and the surf is never more than a barefoot stroll away.

It is the rare place where you can chase barrels at dawn, practise yoga at noon, and watch the stars from a hammock by night — all without leaving home.

  • ✓ Hand-built jungle cabanas & sea-view rooms
  • ✓ Beachfront natural-wood restaurant & bar
  • ✓ Surf lessons, coaching & board rentals
  • ✓ Safaris, yoga, and east-coast sunsets

The Table

Dine under the natural-wood cathedral.

Our open-air restaurant soars three storeys into the sky — a natural-wood and thatch cathedral that glows golden at sunset. Wake to tropical fruit and hoppers; return from the water to fresh rice and curry, the catch of the day, and cold drinks as the sun melts into the bay.

  • ✓ Authentic Sri Lankan rice & curry, hoppers, seafood
  • ✓ Beach BBQ nights, bonfires & live music
  • ✓ Fresh juices, smoothies & sunset cocktails
  • ✓ Vegetarian & vegan options always available

The Place

Sri Lanka's wild east, all to yourself.

We sit near Sangamankanda — the eastern tip of Sri Lanka — where the beaches run empty and white, the swell rolls in clean, and the crowds of the main bay feel a world away. Around us: lagoons full of wildlife, ancient temples, elephant-dotted national parks and the most magnificent sunrises on the island.

  • ✓ Kumana & Lahugala safaris, Pottuvil lagoon trips
  • ✓ Elephant Rock sunsets & temple visits
  • ✓ Airport transfers, tuk-tuks & bikes arranged
